Interpretation of Results

The K-Means algorithm with k=3 successfully segmented the 1,000 customers into three behaviorally distinct groups:

  • Cluster 0 — Budget Shoppers: Young customers (≈18–28 years) with low annual spending ($500–$2,000). Ideal candidates for introductory offers, loyalty programs, and budget product lines.
  • Cluster 1 — Moderate Spenders: Mid-career adults (≈30–45 years) with moderate spending ($3,000–$6,000). Respond well to family bundles, quality products, and mid-tier promotions.
  • Cluster 2 — High Spenders: Established professionals (≈47–65 years) with high annual spending ($7,000–$14,000). Priority segment for premium services, VIP programs, and personalized offers.

This segmentation allows the marketing team to allocate resources efficiently and personalize communication for each customer group — directly improving conversion rates and customer satisfaction.
